# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends banking from their phones around a physical board game. Bank operators need the same mobile experience, with dedicated table controls. Desktop supports larger views.

## Product Purpose

Replace paper money with quick payments, requests and shared banking. Successful sessions keep attention on the table and make balances easy to trust.

## Brand Personality

Bold, clear and playful. Professional banking patterns with game-night personality.

## Anti-references

Long administrative setup forms, competing gradient tiles, inaccessible clickable panels and banking dashboards full of unrelated statistics.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful action within reach.
2. Preserve the colorful bank card as the product signature.
3. Keep optional rules out of basic setup.
4. Confirm payments clearly and surface failures.
5. Keep player and banker workflows distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, labeled icon controls and reduced motion. Player identity always includes names rather than color alone.
